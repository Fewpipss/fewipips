Payouts & Withdrawals

How to Request a Fewpips Payout (Step by Step)

6 min read Updated July 8, 2026 Fewpips Team
Short answer: Once your account meets the payout conditions, you request a payout in two moves. First, register a withdrawal method in your Fewpips CRM profile (Financial tab). Then open the account you want to withdraw from and click Go to Withdrawals. Approved payouts are processed in 24 hours or less, with a $50 minimum.

Getting paid is the whole point of a funded account, and Fewpips keeps the process short. But a payout request only goes through when the account is genuinely eligible, and a lot of traders click too early and see a locked button. This guide walks through when you can request, whether you need KYC, and the exact steps inside the CRM. It applies to both CFD and Future Based CFD accounts.

When Are You Eligible to Request?

Before the withdrawal button unlocks, your account has to clear a short checklist. If any one of these is not met, the button stays locked:

  • The payout cycle and minimum trading days are met. Each account type has a cycle and a required number of active trading days first.
  • The account is in profit. There has to be profit in the cycle to withdraw.
  • Instant accounts are above the 5% growth threshold. An Instant account can take an on-demand reward once it is up 5% or more; below that, the standard 14-day cycle applies.
  • The consistency rule is satisfied. No single day can exceed its share of your total profit (40% on CFD, 50% on Future Based CFD). See the consistency rule explained.

If your button is locked, it is almost always one of the four items above. Our companion guide covers each reason: why the withdrawal button is locked.

Do You Need KYC to Withdraw?

KYC is tied to your deposit method, not your payout.

If you deposited by credit or debit card, KYC is required before depositing.
If you deposited by crypto, no KYC is required at all.

Crypto depositors do not need to complete identity verification to request a withdrawal. There is no separate KYC gate between you and your first payout.

Step 1: Register a Withdrawal Method

You only need to do this once, before your first request. Inside the Fewpips CRM:

  1. On any CRM page, click the "i" icon in the top-right corner. This opens your profile.
  2. Open the Financial tab.
  3. Select Add new withdrawal method and complete the details.
  4. Return to the account you want to withdraw from. The Go to Withdrawals button now appears on the account details page.

Step 2: Request the Payout

With a method saved and the account eligible, open the account details page and click Go to Withdrawals to submit. From there it goes to audit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need KYC to withdraw from Fewpips?

KYC is only required before depositing if you pay by credit or debit card. Crypto deposits do not require KYC, and there is no separate verification gate on the payout itself.

How long does a Fewpips payout take?

Approved payouts are processed in 24 hours or less.

What is the minimum I can withdraw?

The minimum withdrawal is $50, paid via USDT, USDC, ETH or BTC.

Why does my withdrawal button not appear?

You need to register a withdrawal method first, in your profile under the Financial tab. Once saved, the Go to Withdrawals button appears on the account details page, provided the account is also eligible.

Why is my withdrawal button locked even after adding a method?

The account must be eligible: the cycle and minimum trading days met, the account in profit, an Instant account above 5% growth, and the consistency rule satisfied. If any one is missing, the button stays locked.

Requesting a payout is a two-minute job once the account is eligible and a method is saved. Get the withdrawal method in early, check the four eligibility conditions before you click, and the payout itself is fast.
