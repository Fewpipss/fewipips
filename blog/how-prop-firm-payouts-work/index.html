Payouts & Withdrawals

Payout Timelines, Splits and Minimums

5 min read Updated July 8, 2026 Fewpips Team
Short answer: Approved Fewpips payouts are processed in 24 hours or less. The minimum withdrawal is $50, paid via USDT, USDC, ETH or BTC. Profit splits scale with your withdrawal count: Instant runs 70% then 80% then 90%, and 1/2/3-Step runs 80% then 90%.

Three numbers decide what your funded trading actually pays you: how fast the money moves, the minimum you can take, and the share you keep. Here they are for Fewpips, on both CFD and Future Based CFD accounts.

Timeline: 24 Hours or Less

Once your request passes the audit, the payout is processed in 24 hours or less. There is no multi-week wait.

Minimum Withdrawal: $50

The minimum you can withdraw is $50. We pay out via USDT, USDC, ETH and BTC.

Profit Splits: How They Scale to 90%

Your split rises as you complete more successful withdrawals, so it pays to keep a funded account healthy over time.

AccountProfit split progression
Instant70% on the first 3 withdrawals, 80% on the next 3, then 90% from the 7th onward
1-Step / 2-Step / 3-Step80% on the first 3 withdrawals, then 90% from the 4th onward
Worth planning for: the jump to 90% comes faster on challenge accounts (from your 4th withdrawal) than on Instant (from your 7th). Factor that into which product fits your goals. See Instant vs challenge accounts.

Before You Can Withdraw

A payout is only available once the account is eligible: the cycle and minimum trading days met, the account in profit, an Instant account above 5% growth, and the consistency rule satisfied. See how to request a payout for the exact steps, and note that your maximum loss floor trails up and never down as your balance grows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast are Fewpips payouts?

Approved payouts are processed in 24 hours or less.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

$50, paid via USDT, USDC, ETH or BTC.

What profit split does Fewpips pay?

Instant accounts pay 70% on the first three withdrawals, 80% on the next three, then 90% from the seventh. 1-Step, 2-Step and 3-Step accounts pay 80% on the first three, then 90% from the fourth onward.

How do I reach the 90% split fastest?

On 1/2/3-Step accounts the 90% split begins from your fourth successful withdrawal. On Instant accounts it begins from the seventh.
