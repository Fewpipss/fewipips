Per-Trade and Daily Loss Limits Explained

6 min read Updated July 8, 2026 Fewpips Team
Short answer: Two limits protect your account. A per-trade cap of 2% of starting balance applies to every account. A daily loss limit applies only to certain products: Instant accounts have no daily loss limit, CFD 1-Step and 2-Step are 4%, CFD 3-Step is 5%, and Future Based CFD 1-Step is 4%.

Fewpips runs two separate loss limits. Confusing them, or assuming a daily limit exists where it does not, is a common way to misjudge risk. Here is each one with the dollar figures worked out.

Per-Trade Cap: 2% of Starting Balance (All Accounts)

No single trade idea may lose more than 2% of your starting balance. This applies to every account type. A trade idea is all entries on the same symbol and direction opened within a 10-minute window, assessed as one combined result, so you cannot split one bet to dodge the cap. How the Daily Loss Is Measured

The daily loss is calculated as your start-of-day equity minus the lowest floating equity of the day. It covers both open and closed trades, so an unrealized drawdown intraday counts, not just closed losses.

Banking profit widens your room: if you lock in profit earlier in the day, that day's loss limit rises by the profit amount. A good morning literally buys you more margin for error in the afternoon.

Because Instant accounts have no daily loss limit, your main guardrails there are the 2% per-trade cap and the overall maximum loss limit and trailing floor. Mandatory stop-loss and take-profit keep each trade inside the 2% cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Fewpips Instant accounts have a daily loss limit?

No. Instant accounts, both CFD and Future Based CFD, have no daily loss limit. Your guardrails on Instant are the 2% per-trade cap and the overall maximum loss limit.

How is the daily loss limit calculated?

As your start-of-day equity minus the lowest floating equity of the day, covering both open and closed trades. If you bank profit earlier in the day, that day's limit rises by the profit amount.

What is the per-trade loss limit?

2% of your starting balance on every account. For example, $1,000 on a $50,000 account or $2,000 on a $100,000 account.

What is the daily loss limit on the CFD 3-Step?

5%, which is $5,000 on a $100,000 account. CFD 1-Step and 2-Step, and Future Based CFD 1-Step, are 4%.
