Fewpips vs FTMO: Cost, Rules and Profit Split Compared

Short answer: Fewpips is the cheaper entry point, starting at $59 with a 90% profit split and no time limit. FTMO is a larger, longer-established firm with strong brand trust, an 80% base split that scales toward 90%, and higher fees. Both now offer deadline-free evaluations as of 2026. If price and split matter most, Fewpips wins on paper. If track record matters most to you, FTMO has more years behind it.

Cost

Fewpips starts at $59. FTMO's entry challenge sits around $155 for a $10,000 account and rises to roughly $1,080 for a $200,000 account as of 2026.

There is an important nuance on refunds. FTMO returns your challenge fee with your first payout on the funded account, not when you pass the evaluation. So if you pass but never take a payout, you do not get the fee back. Read What the Challenge Fee Actually Pays For for how to think about a challenge fee as an entry cost rather than a deposit.

For a fair cost comparison, look at the total you are likely to spend, which is the fee times the number of attempts you expect to need. A cheaper fee gives you more attempts for the same budget. We cover that math in The Cheapest Prop Firm Challenges in 2026.

Rules and evaluation structure

FTMO runs a 2-Step evaluation and, as of 2026, a newer 1-Step option. On the 2-Step, Phase 1 asks for a 10% profit target and Phase 2 for 5%, with a 5% maximum daily loss and a 10% maximum total loss. FTMO has also asked for a minimum number of trading days in the past, though this has changed over time, so confirm the current rule on FTMO's own site.

Fewpips offers several funding paths, including instant funding and challenge accounts. For the exact Fewpips profit targets, drawdown percentages and phase structure, see Account Types and Sizes and Per-Trade and Daily Loss Limits Explained. 1-Step runs a 10% profit target with a 4% daily loss limit and a trailing maximum loss of 6% to 10% by account size; 2-Step runs 8% then 5% with a 4% daily loss limit and a trailing maximum loss of 8% to 10% by size

Both firms have a consistency rule style requirement designed to reward steady trading over one lucky day. Read the fine print on each, since the exact percentages differ.

Time limits

This used to be a clear difference, but it is not anymore. Fewpips has never had a time limit. FTMO removed its 30 and 60 day deadlines in 2026 and moved to an unlimited trading period on newly purchased accounts. So both let you trade at your own pace now.

If no deadline is a priority for you, you can compare more options in Best Prop Firms With No Time Limit.

Profit split

Fewpips advertises up to a 90% split. FTMO uses an 80% base split that can rise toward 90% through its scaling plan, which rewards traders who stay consistent and profitable over time.

On the headline number, Fewpips is higher from day one. On FTMO, you reach the top tier by earning it over several payout cycles. Which is better depends on whether you value a higher split immediately or are happy to scale into it.

Payouts

Fewpips processes verified crypto payouts within 24 hours after the payout audit. See How to Request a Fewpips Payout and How the Payout Audit Works.

FTMO has a long-running payout system with a strong public track record and a range of withdrawal methods. If you want fast crypto specifically, Fewpips is built around that. If you want a firm with many years of documented payouts, FTMO has that history.

Who each firm suits

  • Choose Fewpips if you want a low entry price, a 90% split from the start, no time limit and fast crypto payouts.
  • Choose FTMO if brand track record and a long public payout history are your top priority and you are comfortable with higher fees.

Neither choice is wrong. They are aimed at slightly different priorities.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fewpips cheaper than FTMO?

Yes, on entry price. Fewpips starts at $59, while FTMO's entry challenge is around $155 for a $10,000 account as of 2026. Remember to also compare the profit split, drawdown rules and how many attempts you expect to need.

Does FTMO have a higher profit split than Fewpips?

Not at the start. FTMO's base split is 80% and scales toward 90% over time. Fewpips advertises up to 90% from the beginning. The gap narrows once an FTMO trader reaches the scaling tier.

Do both firms have no time limit?

Yes, as of 2026. Fewpips never had one, and FTMO removed its deadline in 2026 for newly purchased accounts. Confirm current terms directly, since rules change.

Is FTMO more trustworthy than Fewpips?

FTMO has been around longer and has a larger public payout record, which matters to many traders. Fewpips is newer and competes on price, split and payout speed. Do your own checks on either firm before you buy.
