Instant vs Challenge Accounts: The Differences

6 min read Updated July 8, 2026 Fewpips Team
Short answer: Instant accounts (CFD and Future Based CFD) fund you immediately from purchase, have no daily loss limit, require stop-loss and take-profit on every trade, and use a 5% growth rule for on-demand rewards. Challenge accounts (1/2/3-Step) make you hit a profit target first, apply a daily loss limit, and only require stop-loss and take-profit once funded.

Fewpips gives you two ways to reach a funded account, and they behave differently from the first minute. Here is a clean side-by-side so you can pick the one that fits how you trade.

Instant Accounts in Detail

Instant accounts, both CFD and Future Based CFD, hand you credentials the moment you purchase, with no evaluation to pass. There is no daily loss limit, but a stop-loss and take-profit are mandatory on every trade. The 5% growth rule lets you take an on-demand reward once the account is up 5% or more; below that, the standard 14-day cycle applies.

Challenge Accounts in Detail

On 1/2/3-Step challenges you prove a profit target across one or more phases before funding. A daily loss limit applies, passing rewards are available as an add-on, and the stop-loss and take-profit requirement switches on the moment you become funded.

Quick rule of thumb: choose Instant if you want to trade live immediately and prefer no daily loss limit. Choose a challenge if you would rather prove an evaluation and reach the 90% split faster (from your 4th withdrawal). Sizes for both are in account types and sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Instant accounts have a daily loss limit?

No. Instant accounts, CFD and Future Based CFD, have no daily loss limit. Stop-loss and take-profit are mandatory on every trade instead.

What is the 5% growth rule on Instant accounts?

An Instant account can take an on-demand reward once it is up 5% or more. Below that threshold, the standard 14-day cycle applies.

When does the stop-loss rule apply on a challenge account?

On 1/2/3-Step accounts, stop-loss and take-profit are only required once the account passes and becomes funded, not during the challenge phase.

Do Instant accounts have a passing reward?

No. Instant accounts have no passing reward because there is no evaluation to pass. Passing rewards are available on challenge accounts as an add-on.
