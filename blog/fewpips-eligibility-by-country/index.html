Traders from all over the world want to know one thing before they pay: "Can someone from my country actually use this?" This guide explains how country eligibility works at a prop firm, why some places are off the list, and exactly how to check your own eligibility in a minute.

Why prop firms restrict some countries

A restricted list is not about picking favorites. It comes down to three practical reasons:

  • Sanctions. Firms must follow international sanctions. Certain countries and regions are blocked for everyone in the industry, not just Fewpips.
  • Local financial rules. Some countries have laws that make it hard or risky for a foreign firm to offer trading-style services to residents. Firms restrict those regions to stay compliant.
  • Payment and fraud risk. A handful of regions carry high fraud or payment-abuse rates, so firms limit access to protect honest traders.

Because these reasons are driven by law and risk, the list is fairly standard across the industry, and it can update when rules change.

What "eligible" actually means

Being eligible has two parts, and both matter:

  1. Your country is supported. You are not in a restricted region.
  2. You can complete verification. You can pass identity checks when required, usually at your first payout.

You do not need to be a professional or licensed to trade simulated funds with a prop firm. You trade a virtual account, and if you win inside the rules, you get paid. If you are new to the model, our explainer on what a prop firm is covers the basics before you buy.

If your country is restricted

If you hit a block, here is the honest guidance:

  • Do not use a VPN to fake your location. Getting funded feels good, but a payout audit and identity check can expose a false country later, and that can cost you the payout. It is not worth it.
  • Rules change. Restricted lists update as sanctions and local rules shift. A region that is off today may be supported later.
  • Check the current terms rather than relying on a forum post from last year.

Trading honestly from a supported country is the only path that ends with money in your wallet.
