1.The campaign

The Inner Circle is the official Fewpips affiliate (IB) campaign, operated by Fewpips through the Fewpips CRM. It is the continuation of the existing Fewpips affiliate campaign under a new name: campaign parameters, tracking links and commission terms carry over unchanged.

These terms supplement, and do not replace, the Fewpips IB Partnership Agreement accepted by every approved IB. Where these terms are silent, the IB Partnership Agreement and the Fewpips general Terms & Conditions apply.

2.Eligibility

  • Participants must be 18 years or older.
  • Accounts must be registered under your individual legal name, matching your government-issued ID. Company registrations are not accepted.
  • All accounts are subject to KYC/AML verification.
  • Participation is not available to residents of restricted jurisdictions: Afghanistan, Belarus, Central African Republic, Cuba, Democratic Republic of Congo, Iran, Libya, Mali, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Sudan, South Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen, Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk.
  • Only approved Fewpips IBs earn commissions under this campaign. Approval is granted by Fewpips admin/compliance after the IB request and signed IB Partnership Agreement are reviewed.

3.Joining

  1. Open the campaign link provided in your invitation.
  2. If you are not yet an IB: log in to the Fewpips CRM Dashboard, click the IB tab in the left sidebar, read the program introduction and click "Send IB request".
  3. Review and accept the IB Partnership Agreement and draw your electronic signature.
  4. Wait for admin/compliance approval. After approval you can generate tracking URLs and monitor traffic and commissions in your IB dashboard.

4.Commission structure

Commission rates follow the standard Fewpips IB tier structure. Your tier is determined by your total referral volume on a rolling 30-day basis and reviewed monthly.

Recurring commissions: every new purchase by a referred client generates a new commission.

Multi-level structure: Level 1 covers traders who joined through your personal referral link. Level 2 covers traders referred by your Level 1 referrals. Level 3 covers traders referred by your Level 2 referrals. Registering directly on the Fewpips website without a referral link designates you as a Master IB, able to build a network across all three levels.

5.Commission locking and refunds

A 7-day refund window applies before a commission is locked. If a referred purchase is refunded within that window, the associated commission is not paid. Chargebacks and refunds reverse the associated commission at any time.

6.Payouts

  • Minimum withdrawal: $100.
  • Payout methods: USDT on BEP20, ERC20 or TRC20.
  • Processing time: 24-48 hours.
  • No withdrawal fees from Fewpips. Blockchain network fees apply.

7.Prohibited conduct

  • Self-referrals are prohibited and detected via IP matching.
  • Brand bidding on "Fewpips" keywords (paid search on the brand name) is prohibited.
  • Any activity that breaches the IB Partnership Agreement, including misleading advertising or misrepresentation of Fewpips products, may result in removal from the campaign and forfeiture of unpaid commissions per that agreement.
