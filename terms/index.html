Fewpips is a simulation-based evaluation platform. All trading takes place using virtual funds in a simulated environment. Fewpips is not a broker-dealer, exchange, or investment advisor and does not accept, manage, or hold client deposits in the regulated sense. Any rewards distributed to participants are contractual payments based on simulated performance and do not represent profits derived from live market orders.

3. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and a natural person to register. Fewpips operates an individual trader model and does not accept registrations from companies, corporate entities, or shell organisations. Account credentials must reflect your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

4. One Profile per Person

Each trader is permitted a single Fewpips profile. We use hardware identifiers, IP signatures, and identity hashes to enforce this rule. Attempts to circumvent failed evaluations by creating duplicate accounts will result in immediate termination and forfeiture of any pending rewards.

5. Evaluation & Funded Phases

The challenge phase requires you to meet a defined profit target while observing daily loss limits, maximum loss limits, minimum trading days, and the 40% consistency rule. Pass the phase and you are eligible for a simulated funded account with the same risk parameters. Funded accounts have no profit target.

6. Prohibited Practices

The following activities are strictly prohibited across challenge and funded accounts: latency or arbitrage trading, tick-scalping hyperactivity, copy trading, third-party account management, risk manipulation between linked accounts, system or platform exploitation, news trading during high-impact economic events, and account sharing.

7. Fees & Refunds

Subscription fees paid to Fewpips are strictly for access to our evaluation programs and related tools. A full refund is available within 48 hours of registration, provided no trading has been initiated on the account. A $10 administration charge applies per approved refund. After the first trade, all fees are non-refundable.

Rewards are processed via stablecoin (USDT on BEP20, TRC20, or ERC20 networks). Minimum payout is $50 USD on CFD accounts and $100 USD on Futures accounts. All withdrawal requests undergo a compliance audit and are typically processed in 24 hours or less.
